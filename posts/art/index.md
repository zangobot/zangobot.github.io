## Videogame Dev

### [Metroid Sigma](https://gamejolt.com/games/metroid-sigma/83620)
![Metroid Sigma screenshot](https://m.gjcdn.net/game-screenshot/400/303084-eywpxe9y-v4.webp)

You irrupt in an abandoned facility. Your starship's computer tells you that this research station was closed many years ago, but some days before the station's sensors detected some anomalies. During an early exploration, the computer found that this facility was built in order to complete the Sigma Project, an unknown military plan lead by unkown forces...

- Explore an abandoned facilities, with dozen of rooms!
- Collect as many tanks as you can!
- Be ready to fight creatures from Metroid universe and new enemies!
- During your journey you'll be completely alone in a gloomy atmosphere...

The game runs on Windows, Intel-based macOS and Ubuntu.


### [CogFactory!](https://dermondhase.itch.io/cogfactory)
![CogFactory screenshot](https://img.itch.zone/aW1hZ2UvMzYyNDMyOC8yMTU2OTY5NC5wbmc=/original/N2w0Fx.png)

One day you wake up, and your Crystal friend (yes, a shiny green talking Crystal is your best friend, deal with it) has been captured by Baron Von Kraut!
You, Hero (yes, this is your name, cool innit?), must save your precious friend from being displayed as an amazing hunting trophy in Baron Von Kraut's mansion, by (literally) throwing an endless amount of bombs to the security of the place!
Sneak into the mansion and defeat the powerful security staff, one bomb at the time!

The game runs on Windows, and macOS (both Intel and Apple Silicon)
